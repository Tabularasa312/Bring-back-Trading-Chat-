INDEPENDENT COMMUNITY PETITION

Bring Back the Trading Chat!

A petition calling for the return of the public trading chat in Knight Manager.

What Is This About?

The public trading chat was recently removed from Knight Manager. As a result, many players have lost an important way to exchange trading offers and communicate with one another.

We want this feature to return. The trading chat was more than just a place to trade items: it was also a meeting place for the community.

Why Does It Matter?

The public trading chat made trading between players easier and more direct. Players could share offers, reach others and make new contacts.

The new trading system may be useful, but from our perspective, it does not fully replace the social interaction of a public chat.

We want a solution that improves both trading and communication.

Our Demand

We call for the return of the public trading chat in Knight Manager.

If this is not possible for technical or other reasons, the new trading system can of course remain in place.

However, we would also like an additional, public and convenient way for players to communicate about trading offers.
